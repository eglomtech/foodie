FAQ

Good to Know

Do you offer delivery outside Awasa?

Yes, we deliver within a 15km radius of our main kitchen. Delivery fees adjust based on distance and are shown at checkout.

Can I book a table for a large group?

Absolutely — for parties of 6 or more, call us or message on WhatsApp at least 24 hours ahead so we can prep your table.

Do you cater for dietary restrictions?

We offer vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options across the menu. Just let your server or the order notes know.

Is parking available?

Yes, free parking is available directly in front of the restaurant and in the shared lot next door.
